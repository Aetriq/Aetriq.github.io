About

Located in Ottawa, the capital of Canada, Aetriq Hardware (operating under gordalx) has been a dedicated supplier of specialized electronics since 2018.

What began as a passion for repairing legacy devices has expanded into a comprehensive supply hub. Between maker ecosystems & vintage tech, parts are extensively tested, refurbished, or restored prior to shipping.
