Alex Gordon

Electrical/Computer Engineer

aetriq@protonmail.com | Ottawa, Ontario, Canada

I do everything electronics restoration. I've also been providing component-level repair services since 2016 and expanding into lower level PCB diagnostics in 2019.

Current provided Services

  • Hardware diagnostics (soldering) Board-level fault isolation, trace repair, and SMD component replacement for desktop systems, mobile devices (Apple/Android), and specialized electronic hardware.
  • Computer systems High-performance PC building, including custom hard-loop liquid cooling implementation and firmware-level component optimization.
  • 3D fabrication & CAD design 0.1mm precision CAD modeling (primarily electronic enclosures & mounting brackets). FDM 3D printing services up to 4 colors & more advanced materials including PC, PAHT-CF, ABS, and vapor-smoothing.
  • Vintage restoration Restoration of vintage PCs and CRT monitors. Expertise includes electrolyte cleaning, mainboard recapping, retr0brighting, and data retrieval from legacy media (ZIP, 3.5" Floppy, SYQUEST).
  • CRT Repair I felt this needed it's own section as not too many people provide this service. I do FULL cosmetic restorations, and can fix broken enclosures using large scale 3D printing or plastic welding/finishing.
    I also do recaps, rust removal (incl. implosion band), high voltage SMPS B+ analysis/repair, manual degauss, geometry calibration and yoke convergence/purity calibration.
    In most instances, a broken crt is absolutely repairable. For instances where I may not have the part (like fbt or regular transformers) I can point you in the right direction for parts or rewinding.
