About

Alex Gordon

@AETRIQ // @VEETWO

SPECIALIZATION: E.E. / C.E.
LOCATION: OTTAWA, ON

Specializing in hardware diagnostics, computer systems builds, and electronics restoration. You will frequently see my work under the aliases Aetriq for custom PCBs and 3D designs, and Veetwo as my legacy copyright.

Operating since 2016 in component-level repair, and expanding into specialized PCB-level diagnostics in 2019. I provide precision hardware solutions ranging from legacy data retrieval to advanced micro-soldering and custom fabrication.

Service Modules

Computer/Mobile Hardware

Comprehensive hardware diagnostics, board-level soldering, and repair for desktop PCs, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.

Specialized PC Systems

High-performance system building including hard-loop liquid cooling, full restoration, and component-level optimization.

3D Modeling & Printing

0.1mm/0.1" precision CAD design (mainly electronic enclosures/brackets) alongside 3D printing services with options such as multi-color printing, engineering filaments, and vapor smoothing.

Vintage Tech Restoration

Restoration of vintage PCs and monitors, including retr0brighting, recapping, mainboard repair, and legacy media data retrieval.

I also do full recaps of CRT monitors; this includes circuit analysis & electrolyte cleaning.

Apple Hardware Solutions

Full component-level repair for iPhones & MacBooks, plus firmware modifications & pwning/jailbreaking for legacy and modern devices.

General Electronics

Other things I do include repair of 3D printers, alongside general micro-soldering, diagnostics, and restoration for miscellaneous electronic hardware.
